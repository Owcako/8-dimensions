# Scope of the Product

## 1. In Scope

These are core promises of the product.

- Vertical Road Network
- Car Variety
- City services such as food, drinks, and toilet
- Simple life simulation: hunger, thurst, energy and toilet
- Reward after job

## 2. Out of Scope

These are explicitly not part of the current version.

- Full life simulation
- Rural areas
- Physics car model details

This section matters a lot. It stops fake promises.

# 3. Core Requirements Summary

This section is the short version agents should obey.

## Must Have
- Job -> destination -> reward loop
- Navigation in center screen
- Chaotic roads
- Accurate traffic density/behavior
- Various Chinese EVs
- Hunger/thurst/other simple life simulation
- Physics instead of animations

## Should Have
- Passenger communication
- Detailed/accurate traffic state for reload
- Ordering simulation
- Backstory/context

## Nice To Have
- Cutscenes/storylines
- Realistically modeled scenes/assets
- Detailed physics system
