# Summary

8 Dimensions is a car driving simulator where players act as Didi drivers driving on the vertical landscapes of Chongqing, China, picking up/delivering people at various places. The player should feel enthusiasted because of the vertical roads and the buildings on the mountains.

---

# Purpose

**Primary objective:** Make players astonished by the vertical landscapes of Chonqing.
**Core game loop:** Deliver passengers to their destination -> earn money -> buy various cars.
**Secondary objective:** The player should immerse in a Didi driver life, delivering people day to day non-stop, and work on several side quests on non-workdays like in real life.

# Target Players

For players that:
- Like scenery watching
- Enjoy vast landscapes to drive on
- Enjoy the details of simulation

Not for players that:
- Like exponential progression/upgrades
- Enjoy racing simulation
- Feel satisfied of arcade driving

# Game Loop

1. Goes to a destination in his basic car, the Cycle D
2. Picks up some passengers
3. Delivers them to another destination
4. Earns his money, according to the Didi prices
5. Gathers his money to buy different cars.
